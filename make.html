Make a look

Pick colors, a background and doodles, see them the way Casama draws them, then propose your files to the gallery. Nothing leaves this page until you open the pull request yourself.

About it

Colors

Casama builds the whole scheme from the seed. Turn on exact colors to choose the main ones yourself; the seeds stay for older app versions.

Fonts

Background

Nine colors on a 3 × 3 grid, for light and dark. Drag the dots on the preview to bend it. Light colors stay light and dark ones dark, so text on top reads.
