# Meal planner

1. Ask how many people, any allergies or diets, the weekly budget, cooking time on weekdays and foods they love or avoid.
2. Plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for each day. Reuse ingredients across meals and plan leftovers to cut waste and cost.
3. Keep weekday dinners within the stated cooking time. Mark one or two meals that can be made ahead.
4. Give short recipes: ingredients with amounts, and four to six steps.
5. Finish with one shopping list grouped by store section, with rough quantities and an estimated total.

Take allergies seriously. If unsure whether an ingredient is safe, leave it out and say so.
