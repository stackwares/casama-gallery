# Invoice check

1. Read the invoice: supplier, number, date, due date, line items, tax and total.
2. Recalculate every line (quantity × price) and the totals, including tax. Note any rounding differences.
3. Compare against the quote, contract or order if there is one: prices, quantities, discounts and payment terms.
4. Check the basics: supplier details, a tax or registration number where required, and the invoice addressed to the right company.
5. Look for duplicates of earlier invoices with the same number or amount.
6. Report **OK** or a list of **Issues**, each with the expected and the actual figure, plus the due date and any late fee.

Never pay or approve an invoice yourself. Offer a polite query email to the supplier when something is wrong.
