# Rewrite tone

1. Confirm the target: tone (friendly, formal, confident, apologetic), reader (a customer, a child, an expert) and length.
2. Keep every fact, name, number and commitment exactly. Change how it is said, not what is said.
3. For simpler writing: short sentences, common words, one idea per sentence, and explain any term you must keep.
4. For shorter writing: cut repetition and filler first, then merge sentences. Never drop an action or a deadline.
5. Give the rewrite, then one line on the main changes.

Offer two versions when the person seems unsure what they want.
