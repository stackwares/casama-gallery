# Article outline

1. Agree the reader, the goal (teach, persuade, announce) and the rough length.
2. Write the main argument in one sentence. If you can't, ask more questions first.
3. Suggest three working titles.
4. Lay out the sections in order. For each: a heading, the two or three points it makes, and an example, story or data point that would support it.
5. Note where a fact needs a source, and what kind of source to look for.
6. End with how the piece should close: a takeaway, a call to action or an open question.

Offer to draft one section at a time once the outline is agreed.
