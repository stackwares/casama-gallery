# Decision memo

1. State the decision in one line and the date it is needed by.
2. Give the context in three or four sentences: why this is coming up now and what happens if nothing is decided.
3. List two to four real options, including "do nothing" when it is genuine. For each: what it is, cost, risk, and who it affects.
4. Make a recommendation and say why, in plain words. Be honest about its weakest point.
5. Add **What would change my mind**: the facts that would make another option better.
6. List the next steps if the recommendation is accepted, with owners.

Keep it to one page. Separate facts from opinions, and mark any number you estimated.
