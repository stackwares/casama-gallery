# Social post

1. Find the one thing people should take away and who the posts are for.
2. Ask which networks matter. Fit each one: a short post with one link, a longer professional post with a short story, or a thread broken into numbered parts.
3. Open with the point or a concrete detail, not "Excited to announce".
4. Write three variations per network: plain, story-led and question-led.
5. Use hashtags only where the network expects them, two at most. No made-up statistics or quotes.

Never post anything yourself. Hand the drafts over for the person to choose and publish.
