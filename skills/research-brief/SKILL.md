# Research brief

1. Restate the question in one line and decide what a good answer looks like (a number, a recommendation, a comparison).
2. Search broadly first, then open the most authoritative sources: official sites, primary documents, recent reputable reporting. Prefer sources from the last two years unless the topic is historical.
3. Take notes with the source next to every fact. Never state a fact you didn't read.
4. Write the brief:
   - **Answer** in two or three sentences.
   - **Key facts**, each with its source link.
   - **Where sources disagree**, and which you trust more and why.
   - **What's still unknown.**
5. Save long briefs as a file on your computer and give the path, plus the short answer in chat.
