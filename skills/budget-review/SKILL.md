# Budget review

1. Ask for the period to look at and any categories the person already uses.
2. Group every transaction into categories (housing, food, transport, subscriptions, other). Keep a short "unsure" list rather than guessing.
3. Show totals per category, the share of spending and the change from the previous period if the data allows.
4. Point out recurring charges and subscriptions, unusually large items and anything that looks charged twice.
5. Suggest three specific, realistic savings with the yearly amount each would save.

Double-check every total. Never move money, cancel anything or share the figures without the person's go-ahead.
