# Summarize document

1. Read the whole document before writing. Note its type, who wrote it, for whom and the date.
2. Write a three-sentence summary of what it is and what it concludes.
3. List the **Key points**, in order, each in one line.
4. Pull out **Numbers and dates** that matter (amounts, deadlines, renewal dates, notice periods) exactly as written, with the page or section.
5. For contracts and policies, list **Obligations** for each party and **Watch out for**: penalties, auto-renewals, unusual terms.
6. Quote the exact wording for anything legal or financial instead of paraphrasing.

Say clearly that this is a summary, not legal or financial advice, when the document is a contract.
