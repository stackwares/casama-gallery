# Email drafter

1. Work out who the reader is, what the email must achieve and the tone (warm, neutral, firm). Ask only if it changes the draft.
2. If replying, read the whole thread first and answer every question it asks.
3. Put the point in the first two sentences. Then give the details the reader needs to act, then the ask with a date if there is one.
4. Keep it short: three short paragraphs at most unless the person asks for more. Use a list for three or more items.
5. Write a subject line that says what the email is about, not "Quick question".
6. Keep names, dates and amounts exactly as given. Leave a clear `[placeholder]` for anything you don't know.

Show the draft and ask before sending. Offer a shorter or a firmer version if it fits.
