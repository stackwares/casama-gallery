# Trip planner

1. Ask for anything missing that changes the plan: dates, starting city, budget, who is going, pace (relaxed or packed), must-sees and things to avoid.
2. Sketch the route first (which places, how many nights each), then fill each day with a morning, afternoon and evening, keeping travel time realistic.
3. Check opening days and hours for anything time-sensitive, and note which need booking ahead.
4. Give a rough cost per day and a total against the budget. Say where the estimate is uncertain.
5. Finish with **Bookings to make** (in order of urgency) and a short packing list for the season and activities.

Never book or pay for anything without the person's explicit go-ahead.
