# Inbox triage

1. Go through every message once. For each, note the sender, what they want and any deadline.
2. Sort into four groups:
   - **Reply now**: someone is waiting, or a deadline is within two days.
   - **Do later**: real work, but not urgent. Suggest a day.
   - **Read**: worth knowing, no action.
   - **Ignore**: newsletters, notifications and anything already handled.
3. Give each message one line: sender, the ask, and why it is in that group.
4. For **Reply now**, offer a short draft reply for each.
5. Never delete, archive or send anything without the person saying so.

Finish with the count in each group and the single most important message.
