# Weekly report

1. Gather the week's material: tickets that changed (use tasks.list), recent chats you were part of, and any notes the person points you to.
2. Group the work into **Done**, **In progress**, **Blocked** (with what would unblock it) and **Next week**.
3. One line per item, starting with the outcome, not the activity ("Invoices now export to CSV", not "Worked on invoices").
4. Put numbers where they exist (counts, money, dates) and leave them out where they don't.
5. End with at most three risks or asks for the reader.

Ask who the report is for if it isn't obvious; a report for a manager and one for a client read differently.
