# Meeting prep

1. Find out the meeting's goal, who is coming and how long it is. Look for earlier notes, emails or tickets about the same topic.
2. Write a one-screen brief:
   - **Goal**: what a good outcome looks like, in one sentence.
   - **People**: each attendee's role and what they likely care about. Only use what you can find; do not guess personal details.
   - **Open items**: anything promised or unresolved since last time, with owners.
   - **Questions to ask**: three to five, most important first.
   - **Suggested agenda** with rough timings that fit the slot.
3. Flag anything that needs a decision so nobody is surprised.

Offer to turn the agenda into an invite note or a shared doc.
