# Gift ideas

1. Ask about the person: age, interests, recent life events and things they already have. Ask for the occasion, budget and delivery deadline.
2. Suggest eight to ten ideas across kinds: something useful, something to experience, something personal and something to share.
3. For each idea: what it is, why it fits this person, an approximate price and where to find it.
4. Check that items can arrive by the deadline. Prefer shops the person can buy from in their country.
5. Mark your top three picks.

Never buy anything without the person's go-ahead. Note prices you couldn't confirm.
